# MultiMini Cookie and Local Storage Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026.
Publisher: Mansoor Alakoozai, Ontario, Canada.

## Cookies and tracking
The Windows desktop app does not set browser cookies, use advertising identifiers, embed web pages or videos, or include app analytics or advertising trackers. It does not contain a browser-based sign-in or checkout. There is no cookie preference banner in this build because there are no app cookies or optional tracking technologies to accept or reject.

## Necessary local files
MultiMini stores settings, workspaces, agreement acceptance, capture buffers and optional screenshots to provide the functions you choose. These are ordinary local files or memory buffers, not browser cookies. Their locations, retention and deletion options are described in the Privacy Policy. Accepting the agreement is not consent to advertising, analytics or unrelated data use.

## External services
Update checks contact GitHub and download hosts. Opening a GitHub page in your own browser subjects that visit to GitHub's policies and your browser's cookie settings. Websites displayed inside captured windows also have their own practices; MultiMini does not manage their cookies. You can use the browser's privacy controls for those sites.

## Future changes
If optional analytics, embeds, accounts, payments or tracking are introduced, the publisher must reassess the policy and consent requirements before enabling them. This policy describes this desktop build, not a separate website or GitHub's services.
