If you paid a distributor or bought a related service, contact the seller identified on your receipt to request cancellation, a refund or information about the applicable purchase terms. The absence of an in-app refund mechanism does not remove a seller's legal obligations or your mandatory consumer rights. This policy does not declare every sale final or promise a refund from a third party.

## Future paid offers
Any future paid offer needs its own clear price, seller identity and contact details, delivery terms, cancellation and refund conditions before purchase. This document does not establish terms for an unconfirmed future offer.

## Support
General app issues can be reported at https://github.com/MaDaAi/MultiMini/issues. Issues are public: do not post receipts, card numbers, addresses, passwords or private captures. A confidential payment or privacy support channel has not been published in this build.
