# Help with MultiMini

## Get started
Choose a view at the top, select Add and choose a source type. Configure its window, display or image. Select a source on the left to adjust its opacity and size on the right. The main controls remain in their usual positions.

## Arrange sources
Drag a source in the preview to move it; drag its edge handles to resize. Hold Shift while resizing to stretch and Alt while dragging to bypass snapping. Use Reset size and position to undo a transform. Drag rows to reorder layers. Double-click a source to configure it; F2 renames it and Delete removes the selected source. Source removal does not delete the original image file.

## Views and source controls
Right-click a view tab to rename, duplicate or delete it. The eye beside a source controls visibility. The mouse icon includes a source in input switching. The source toolbar provides Add, Remove, Duplicate, Show or hide, Move up and Move down. Use Tab to reach toolbar buttons; their accessible names describe each icon.

## Multitask and screenshots
Start multitasking enters the overlay. Default shortcuts are Right Ctrl to switch input, Pause to swap visual layers and End to exit. Insert saves the full view and Ctrl+Shift+F12 selects a screenshot region, during multitask mode. Your configured shortcuts may differ: check Settings > Controls. Region capture offers Copy, Save, Copy and Save, and Cancel.

Gallery lets you copy, keep or delete saved screenshots and open their file location. Unkept screenshots expire after seven days when cleanup runs. Keep prevents automatic deletion. Clipboard copies and OS backups are outside the app's control.

## Keyboard access
Tab and Shift+Tab move through controls. Space activates focused buttons; use arrow keys in lists, sliders and dropdowns. On a focused settings tab, use Left or Right to switch pages. Tab into a document to read with arrow keys or Page Down, then Tab to leave. Opacity and Size percentage buttons accept typed values; Enter commits and Escape cancels the value edit. In a shortcut recorder, Escape cancels recording.

## Capture troubleshooting
Reselect a window if its saved target cannot reconnect. Keep image source files in their original location. Protected content, exclusive fullscreen apps and some games may not capture. Try windowed or borderless mode and review the target application's rules. Windows Graphics Capture compatibility does not imply a universal game hook or permission to bypass protections. Capture rate and latency depend on the system and source.

## Privacy, safety and support
The Privacy, Terms, Cookies and Refunds tabs explain local storage and external update traffic. No support or marketing submission form is built into this app. Publisher: Mansoor Alakoozai, Ontario, Canada. Public support: https://github.com/MaDaAi/MultiMini/issues. Include the app version and steps to reproduce a problem; remove personal data and private captures first. Do not post confidential privacy requests in public issues.

Exit multitask mode before sensitive operations and confirm which source receives input. Screen-reader coverage of the visual composition and mouse-only transform gestures is limited; no full accessibility certification is claimed.
