MultiMini for Mac - untested preview

1. Drag MultiMini into Applications.
2. Open it. If macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "cannot verify" it:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to MultiMini. Confirm once.
   - macOS 14 or older: Control-click (right-click) MultiMini in Applications,
     choose Open, then Open again.
   This happens because this preview is not signed with a paid Apple
   Developer ID. You only need to do it once per version.
3. When asked, allow MultiMini in:
   - Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording (to capture windows)
   - Privacy & Security > Accessibility (to send clicks/keys to the selected app)
   Quit and reopen MultiMini after granting them.

If macOS says the app is "damaged", run this once in Terminal, then open it:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MultiMini.app

This build was made without access to a Mac. Please report what works and
what doesn't at https://github.com/MaDaAi/MultiMini/issues
